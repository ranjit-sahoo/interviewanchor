Privacy and data controls

This policy covers the free-practice Android release of InterviewAnchor (version 1.0.8), operated by Ranjit Kumar Sahoo. Updated November 10, 2026.

On your device

Your practice notes, marked questions and downloaded question pack stay in your browser or app storage. They are not sent to our server. There is no account, AI processing, advertising, analytics tracker, microphone recording or payment in this release.

Network requests

The app downloads its pages and question pack over HTTPS. Render (hosting) and Cloudflare (network security) may process technical request data such as IP address to deliver and protect the service. We do not derive your location, build an advertising profile or sell your data.

Delete or export

Use "Export my notes" to download your practice data. Use "Clear local notes and progress" to remove it from this device. Clearing browser or app storage also removes it. There is no server account to delete.

Support

If you email support, your email address and message are used to answer your request. Do not send private resumes or identity documents. Contact: appsupportdesk.in@gmail.com.

Audience and changes

This app is intended for adults 18 and older preparing for work interviews. Future features that change data handling will require an updated policy before release.
