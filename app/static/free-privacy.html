Privacy and data controls

This policy covers the free-practice Android release of InterviewAnchor (version 1.0.9), operated by Ranjit Kumar Sahoo. Updated November 12, 2026.

On your device

Your practice notes, marked questions and downloaded question pack stay in your browser or app storage. They are not sent to our server. There is no account, AI processing, advertising, analytics tracker, microphone recording or payment in this release.

Network requests and server logs

The app downloads its pages and question pack over HTTPS. The service is hosted by Render and delivered through Cloudflare's network. Like most web services, our hosting keeps technical request logs - such as IP address, requested page, time and response status - to run, debug and protect the service. On our current Render plan, Render retains these server logs for up to 7 days before they automatically expire. We do not keep a separate copy and do not forward logs to any other logging service. Render and Cloudflare also process request data under their own privacy policies, whose retention we do not control: Render privacy policy (Render log retention) and Cloudflare privacy policy. We do not derive your location, build an advertising profile or sell your data.

Delete or export

Use "Export my notes" to download your practice data. Use "Clear local notes and progress" to remove it from this device. Clearing browser or app storage also removes it. There is no server account to delete.

Support

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to answer your request. Please do not send resumes, identity documents or other sensitive personal information. Support emails are stored in our support mailbox on Gmail, and a copy is forwarded to the operator's Gmail inbox. We keep a support conversation only while it is needed to handle your request, or longer only where a specific legal or security reason applies, and then delete it. Contact: appsupportdesk.in@gmail.com.

Audience and changes

This app is intended for adults 18 and older preparing for work interviews. Future features that change data handling will require an updated policy before release.
