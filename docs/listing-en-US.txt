PLANNED YEARLY AI PLAN

Daily/monthly calls, feature limits, request sizes and annual processing allowances all apply; the first reached limit pauses new AI calls. See Plans before any purchase. Google Play shows the final localized price and terms before payment.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Accounts are optional for free practice. Sign in for saved history and account tools. Export or delete your saved account data from the app. Recruiter screening has candidate notice and deletion controls. This staged release has no ads.

AI results, scores and salary estimates can be wrong. This is a coaching tool, not a hiring guarantee or automated hiring decision system. Check facts before using generated content. For adults18+.
